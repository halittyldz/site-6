DAF – known for efficiency, reliability, and comfort

DAF trucks are known among drivers and transport companies as powerful and comfortable vehicles with excellent fuel consumption and high availability. From distribution to long-haul transport, DAF offers a balanced mix of efficiency, driving comfort, and reliability. This makes the brand a favorite in European transport.

Premium DAF styling by Solar Guard

A truck of this caliber deserves a look that matches its performance. Solar Guard develops premium styling products for every DAF generation – from CF Euro 6 and XF 106 to the latest NGD models (XD, XF, XG, XG+). As one of the first suppliers of styling solutions for the new generation of DAF trucks, we have played an important role in shaping the current market standard in terms of design and fit.
